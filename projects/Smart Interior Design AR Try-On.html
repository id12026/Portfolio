A Smart System for Personalized Interior Design & AR Try-On

  • Tech Stack: Python, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, ResNet, YOLOv8, Stable Diffusion, OpenCV, WebXR, React.js, FastAPI, SQLite, Generative AI
  • Timeline: Jul 2025 – Oct 2025
  • Github URL: Project Link

Addressed the challenge of 90% of users struggling to visualize furniture before purchase by building an AI-powered try-before-you-buy platform with 3D AR visualization.

Built an ETL pipeline, trained ResNet for room classification, and YOLOv8 for furniture detection. The system accurately identifies room types and detects furniture items in uploaded images, enabling intelligent recommendations.

Integrated Generative AI for personalized recommendations and layout optimization. Using Stable Diffusion and advanced AI models, the system generates customized interior design suggestions based on user preferences and room characteristics.

Delivered a smart try-before-you-buy feature with 3D visualization and user feedback integration to enhance decision-making. The WebXR-powered AR interface allows users to virtually place furniture in their space before making purchase decisions, significantly improving customer satisfaction and reducing returns.
